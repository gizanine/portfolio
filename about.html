The story
Biography

Long before design school, there was a blog. A young Giulia, already restless with ideas, carved out her own corner of the internet — writing, curating, composing layouts on Tumblr and whatever platform would let her make things look just right. She didn't know it then, but she was already designing.

That instinct — to shape how something is seen and felt — never left. It only grew sharper. After graduating in Graphic Design from UNICID, she turned that lifelong intuition into craft: visual identities, graphic systems, and brand languages built on the quiet power of restraint. Her work doesn't shout. It stays with you.

Giulia believes that a designer who stops being curious stops growing. So she keeps feeding herself — new languages, new cities, new dance studios, new pages in books she carries everywhere. Every experience outside the studio finds its way back in: the rhythm of a choreography, the visual grammar of a foreign city, the way a sentence lands in another tongue.

She's drawn to challenges that ask more of her than her comfort zone allows. Whether it's mastering a new language, pushing through a hard workout, or rethinking a brand from the ground up — she shows up fully, ready to be changed by the process.
