Overview

Freshdive is a surf and water-sports lifestyle brand built on the feeling of breaking the surface - that sharp, electric moment when cold water hits warm skin. The challenge was to capture that raw, kinetic energy in a visual identity that stays versatile across apparel, gear, and digital channels without losing its edge.

The logo leads with a cresting wave rendered in fluid, hand-crafted strokes, paired with a bold, grounded wordmark in deep black. The pairing is deliberate: the mark carries movement and optimism while the typography anchors it with confidence. Cyan and aqua tones evoke open water and clear skies without resorting to tired tropical cliches.

From wetsuit tags to social content, every touchpoint was designed for someone who lives between sessions - equally at home on the beach and in the city. Freshdive is for those who don't just surf the water, but carry that feeling everywhere they go.
