Eliza Gomes

Eliza Gomes is a clinical psychopedagogy brand designed to feel calm, human, and reassuring from the very first point of contact. The identity was created for a professional practice where trust matters deeply, so every visual choice balances warmth with clarity.

The lotus-inspired mark, soft coral accent, and gentle sage palette create a visual language connected to care, growth, and emotional balance. Paired with delicate typography and refined applications, the brand feels personal without becoming overly decorative.

Across stationery, signage, and client-facing materials, the system keeps the experience consistent and welcoming. Eliza Gomes was built to communicate professionalism with softness: a quiet identity for meaningful work.
