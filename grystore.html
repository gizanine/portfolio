Gry Store

Gry Store is a feminine boutique identity designed to feel elegant, modern, and softly luxurious. The visual direction blends romantic details with clean editorial spacing, creating a brand that feels refined but still warm and approachable.

The project explores how a fashion store can feel premium across packaging, social media, digital presence, and printed brand materials. Soft pinks, delicate floral elements, and minimalist compositions create a cohesive boutique experience.

From shopping bags and tags to social content and e-commerce visuals, the system was created to support a fashion brand that feels polished, memorable, and ready for online growth.
