Overview

Binge TV is an editorial design project built around the energy of contemporary entertainment culture. The concept treats television not as background noise, but as a shared visual language shaped by fandom, identity, humor, and the people who make pop culture feel personal.

The cover system leans into oversized typography, soft color tension, and confident image placement to create the feeling of a collectible entertainment issue. Inside, the layout balances expressive headlines with quieter reading moments, giving interviews, features, and image-led spreads enough room to breathe.

From the cover story to the feature spread, the magazine was designed to feel polished but immediate: bold enough for a newsstand, flexible enough for recurring issues, and warm enough to invite the reader into the world of the story.
